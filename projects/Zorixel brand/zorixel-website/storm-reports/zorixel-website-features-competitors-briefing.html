The 60-Second Summary

Modern 2026 developer platforms are moving away from traditional npm dependencies toward copy-and-paste micro-interaction libraries (Magic UI, Aceternity UI) combined with utility-gated lead magnets. To win against generic component hubs, Zorixel must combine a stunning 3D kinetic presentation (3D Helical Spiral, GSAP 3-digit rolling counter preloader) with the 5-Tool Modern Backend Stack (Clerk, Supabase, UploadThing, Resend, Trigger.dev).
