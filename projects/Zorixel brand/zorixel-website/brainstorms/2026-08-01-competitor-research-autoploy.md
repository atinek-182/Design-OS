- **Dual-Market Agency Model**: Separating lead funnels and proposals for Domestic (India) vs. International (Global) clients based on local purchasing power while maintaining brand consistency.

- **Full-Stack AI Automation Engine**: Combining custom Python/n8n backend agentic workflows with custom TypeScript/Next.js frontend dashboards.

## 5-Persona Adversarial Roast Council Audit (`/roast`)

### Persona 1: The Contrarian (Skeptical CFO & Risk Manager)
- **Critique**: Dual-market pricing leaks and burning 80 hours on cheap Indian hand-holding clients.
- **Strategic Guardrail**: Implement strict intake filtering. Never publish INR prices on global pages. Set hard scope boundaries (maximum 15-20 hours per domestic sprint) to ensure profitability while building early case studies.

### Persona 2: The Expansionist (Scale & Ecosystem Strategist)
- **Critique**: Multi-platform content fragmentation risk across 5 channels simultaneously.
- **Strategic Guardrail**: YouTube is designated as the single source of truth for content creation. Weekly 1 long-form YouTube build video is re-formatted into 1 LinkedIn post, 1 X thread, 1 Reddit breakdown, and 1 IG carousel. Zero extra writing time.

### Persona 3: The Logician (Architectural & Operational Systems Lead)
- **Critique**: Treating web design as dead weight ignores that AI agents need frontend UIs.
- **Strategic Guardrail**: Position full-stack frontend web design as ZORIXEL's ultimate competitive moat over no-code Make/n8n coders. Every automation package includes a clean, custom web portal or client dashboard.

### Persona 4: The Researcher (Market & Competitive Analyst)
- **Critique**: Autoploy's single-page VSL lander lacks technical depth and proof of full-stack software development.
- **Strategic Guardrail**: Exploit Autoploy's weakness by showcasing live interactive web UI dashboards, multi-agent Python scripts, and verified client case studies.

### Persona 5: The Buyer (Enterprise Client / Decision Maker)
- **Critique**: Unclear hosting liabilities and API key cost management.
- **Strategic Guardrail**: Enforce strict Client-Owned Infrastructure. Clients sign up for their own Anthropic/OpenAI and hosting accounts; ZORIXEL executes the build sprint and hands over complete ownership.
