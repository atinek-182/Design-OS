# Unleashing Best — Victor Work Executive Aesthetics & 6 Theme Runtimes

`Unleashing Best` (`https://unleashingbest.com/`) is an Orpetron Site of the Month winning executive learning platform designed and engineered by **Victor Work**.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Victor Work Pedigree

- **Author & Creative Director**: Victor Work
- **Honors**: Orpetron Site of the Month, Muzli & Frontend Horse Masterclass Showcase
- **Executive Persona**: Soft pastel canvas fills (`#D6E2DE`, `#FDE3D5`, `#CCE1E8`) reducing visual eye strain, paired with high-contrast dark Charcoal typography (`#191919`) and 6 dynamic runtime theme transitions.
