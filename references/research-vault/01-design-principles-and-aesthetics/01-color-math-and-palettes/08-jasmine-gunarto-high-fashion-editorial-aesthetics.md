# Jasmine Gunarto — High-Fashion Editorial Aesthetics & Color Tokens

`Jasmine Gunarto` (`https://jasminegunarto.com/`) is an Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD, April 22 2026) and Developer Award-winning portfolio platform designed by 2D motion designer **Jasmine Gunarto** and engineered by developer **Diar Adelita Sury**.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Studio Pedigree

- **Honors**: Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD) + Developer Award + Honorable Mention.
- **Design Archetype**: High-Fashion Editorial Portfolio & Kinetic Motion Design Gallery.
- **Visual Persona**: Obsidian Black background paired with Cream Off-White typography, Deep Earth Brown accents, and generous editorial whitespace.

---

## High-Fashion Editorial Color Specification

| Color Role | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Obsidian Black Canvas** | `#090909` | Viewport background fill |
| **Cream Off-White** | `#EBEAE4` | Editorial headline text & primary typography |
| **Deep Earth Brown** | `#341C09` | Hover states, category tags & subtle borders |
| **Pure White** | `#FFFFFF` | Active modal container fills & crisp accents |
