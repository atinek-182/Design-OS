# Follow Art — Electric Cobalt Blue Aesthetics & Rebellious Art Branding

`FOLLOW.ART` is an award-winning digital infrastructure platform created by design studio Vide Infra for contemporary curators and artists.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Multi-Award Pedigree

- **Honors**: Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD, Jan 29 2026), FWA of the Day, CSS Design Awards WOTD.
- **Design Persona**: Rebellious contemporary art identity combining high-contrast electric blue canvases with bold editorial typography.
- **"The Card" Digital Identity**: QR code digital portfolio & mobile wallet infrastructure engineered for international art fairs.

---

## Electric Cobalt Color Token Specification

| Color Role | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Electric Cobalt Blue** | `#0000FF` | Primary brand canvas background & hero highlight fill |
| **Obsidian Black** | `#000000` | High-contrast typography & structural container fills |
| **Pure White** | `#FFFFFF` | Primary headlines, card surfaces & cursor indicators |
| **Soft Periwinkle** | `#A3A3FF` | Secondary metadata labels & subtle border accents |
