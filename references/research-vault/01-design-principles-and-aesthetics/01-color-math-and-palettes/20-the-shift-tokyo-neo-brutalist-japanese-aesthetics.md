# The Shift Tokyo — Japanese Neo-Brutalist Aesthetics & Electric Coral Tokens

`The Shift Tokyo` (`https://theshift.tokyo/en/`) is an experimental digital agency and creative collective based in Tokyo, Japan.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Tokyo Collective Pedigree

- **Creative Collective**: The Shift (Tokyo, Japan)
- **Core Mission**: *"Created for things and people desiring a shift - a movement, a change - in business, organization, space, product, or service."*
- **Visual Persona**: Pitch Dark canvas paired with Off-White surface cards, Electric Coral callout accents (`#FE4249`), and real-time Tokyo clock HUD anchors.

---

## Neo-Brutalist Color Specification

| Color Role | OKLCH Code | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pitch Dark Void** | `oklch(0.10 0.005 240)` | `#0B0B0B` | Viewport background fill |
| **Off-White Surface** | `oklch(0.97 0.005 90)` | `#F5F5F5` | Light theme canvas & card container fills |
| **Electric Coral Accent** | `oklch(0.64 0.23 24)` | `#FE4249` | Custom cursor glow, active route indicators & CTA highlights |
| **Subdued Slate Text** | `oklch(0.50 0.005 240)` | `#7F7F7F` | Subdued metadata text & grid lines |
