# Outfit Hello Hello — Editorial Streetwear Aesthetics & Electric Red Tokens

`Outfit` (`https://outfit.hellohello.is/`) is an Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD) winning digital apparel showcase designed and built by Montevideo-based studio **++hellohello**.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Studio Pedigree

- **Creative Agency**: ++hellohello (Montevideo, Uruguay)
- **Studio Honors**: 30+ global awards across Awwwards, FWA, and Framer Awards
- **Core Aesthetic**: Editorial streetwear design featuring high-contrast color blocking, massive 45vw display typography, and clean line borders (`border-black/10`).
- **Visual Persona**: Heritage Cream canvas (`#EDE4DD`) paired with vibrant Electric Red callout blocks (`#FF0001`) and Jet Black display text (`#000000`).

---

## Streetwear Color Specification

| Color Role | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Heritage Cream Canvas** | `#EDE4DD` | Primary background fill & card container background |
| **Electric Red Accent** | `#FF0001` | Hero banner block fill, pill buttons & active hover states |
| **Jet Black Typography** | `#000000` | Display headline typography & crisp border lines |
| **Translucent Glass Surface** | `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.05)` | Card overlay borders & backdrop blurs |
