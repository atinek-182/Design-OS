# Merci Michel — French Gamified Surrealism & Neon Crimson Palette

`Merci-Michel` (`https://mercimichel.com/`) is an elite digital production and gamification studio based in Paris, France.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Hall of Fame Pedigree

- **Studio Location**: Paris, France
- **Awwwards Hall of Fame**: 2 Site of the Year (SOTY) awards, 6 Site of the Month (SOTM) awards, 41 Site of the Day (SOTD) awards
- **Iconic Client Campaigns**: Lacoste, Hermès, Chanel, Cdiscount
- **Core Philosophy**: *"Creativity Drives Technology"* — technology is never used for its own sake, but serves to elevate playful narrative storytelling and emotional brand connection.
- **Visual Persona**: Obsidian Void canvas paired with Deep Indigo Slate surface elevations, Neon Crimson accents (`#FF3366`), and Royal Electric Purple highlights (`#A855F7`).

---

## Gamified Surrealism Color Specification

| Color Role | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Obsidian Void Canvas** | `#0A0B10` | Deep background viewport fill |
| **Deep Indigo Slate Surface** | `#12141D` | Translucent card containers & HUD drawer fills |
| **Neon Crimson Accent** | `#FF3366` | Primary hover states, active indicators & CTA accents |
| **Royal Electric Purple Accent** | `#A855F7` | Secondary WebGL particle bloom highlights |
| **Luminous White Typography** | `#F8FAFC` | Crisp headline display & high-contrast UI copy |
