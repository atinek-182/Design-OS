# Truck 'n Roll — Heavy Industrial Tour Aesthetics & High-Visibility Red

`Truck 'n Roll` (`https://trucknroll.com/`) is an Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD, June 3 2026) winning digital platform engineered by **Locomotive®** (Montreal, QC, Canada).

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Studio Pedigree

- **Creative Agency**: Locomotive® (Montreal, QC, Canada)
- **Honors**: Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD), Developer Award Winner
- **30-Year Legacy**: North America's premier live entertainment and stadium concert tour logistics carrier.
- **Visual Persona**: Dark Charcoal background (`#0E0F12`) evoking night stage load-ins, paired with Tour Crimson Red (`#E53935`), Caution Yellow (`#FFD54F`), and structured road case grid borders (`#27272A`).

---

## Industrial Tour Color Specification

| Color Role | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Dark Charcoal Void** | `#0E0F12` | Viewport background fill |
| **Road Case Grid Border** | `#27272A` | Structured container outlines |
| **Tour Crimson Red** | `#E53935` | Primary action states & status indicators |
| **Caution Yellow Accent** | `#FFD54F` | Technical metadata tags & sub-headings |
| **Heavy White Display Text** | `#FFFFFF` | Druk condensed headlines |
