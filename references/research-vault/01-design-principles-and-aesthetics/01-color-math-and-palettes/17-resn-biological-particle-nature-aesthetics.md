# Resn — Biological 3D Particle Aesthetics & Chlorophyll Green Palette

`Pioneer® — Corn Revolutionized` (`https://cornrevolution.resn.global/#`) is a WebGL biological showcase crafted by **Resn** (Wellington, New Zealand & Amsterdam, Netherlands).

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Resn Pedigree

- **Creative Agency**: Resn (New Zealand & Netherlands)
- **Agency Honors**: 14-time Awwwards Agency of the Year, Site of the Year (SOTY), FWA Hall of Fame
- **Client Partnership**: Pioneer (Bader Rutter)
- **Visual Persona**: Dark Moss background paired with Chlorophyll Green particle highlights (`#22C55E`), Harvest Gold accents (`#EAB308`), and bioluminescent cell shaders.

---

## Biological Nature Color Specification

| Color Role | OKLCH Code | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dark Moss Void** | `oklch(0.08 0.02 135)` | `#070A06` | Deep viewport background fill |

| **Chlorophyll Green Accent** | `oklch(0.65 0.22 145)` | `#22C55E` | Active particle bloom highlights & primary CTA states |
| **Harvest Gold Accent** | `oklch(0.78 0.18 85)` | `#EAB308` | Secondary chapter tags & biological node indicators |
