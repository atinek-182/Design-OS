# TRIONN Agency — AI-Powered Studio Aesthetics & Indigo Glow Tokens

`TRIONN®` (`https://trionn.com/`) is an Awwwards-winning AI-Powered Digital Studio specializing in UI/UX design, WebGL interactive experiences, and fullstack engineering.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & AI Studio Pedigree

- **Studio**: TRIONN® AI-Powered Digital Studio
- **Core Mission**: Delivering AI-driven web applications, interactive 3D stages, and premium digital products.
- **Visual Persona**: Obsidian Black background (`oklch(0.12 0.01 260)` / `#0D0E12`) paired with Card Surface fills (`oklch(0.16 0.02 260)` / `#15171E`), Indigo Glow callouts (`oklch(0.55 0.22 275)` / `#6366F1`), and Cyan accents (`oklch(0.70 0.18 200)` / `#06B6D4`).
