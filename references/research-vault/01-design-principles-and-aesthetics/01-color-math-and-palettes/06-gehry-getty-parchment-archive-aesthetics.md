# Gehry Getty — Parchment Archive Aesthetics & Museum Color Palette

*Sculpting Harmony: Frank Gehry and the Walt Disney Concert Hall* (`https://gehry.getty.edu/`) is an interactive digital exhibition built by legendary studio **Resn** for the **Getty Research Institute**.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Resn Studio Pedigree

- **Honors**: Awwwards Site of the Day, FWA of the Month, Webby Award Winner.
- **Design Persona**: Museum archival elegance translating Frank Gehry's deconstructivist paper models into interactive 3D WebGL canvases.
- **Atmospheric Texture**: Warm parchment paper background surfaces paired with titanium metallic sail accents and charcoal typography.

---
