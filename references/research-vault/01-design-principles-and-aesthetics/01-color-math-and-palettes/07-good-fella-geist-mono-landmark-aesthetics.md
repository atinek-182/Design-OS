# Good Fella — Geist Mono Monospaced Aesthetics & Electric Red Tokens

`Good Fella` (good-fella.com) is an Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD, March 18 2026) and CSS Design Awards WOTD-winning frontend development studio based in Munich, Germany, founded by brothers **Julian and Adrian Fella**.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Annnimate Pedigree

- **Honors**: Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD) + Developer Award + Honorable Mention.
- **Industry Pedigree**: Creators of the **Annnimate** component library and **The Content Architecture** (Next.js + Sanity CMS architecture featured on Codrops).
- **Digital Landmark Persona**: Obsidian dark background paired with monospaced Vercel `Geist Mono` typography and electric red highlight accents (`#FF0000`).

---

## Digital Landmark Color Specification

| Color Role | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Obsidian Dark Canvas** | `#0A0A0A` | Main body viewport background fill |
| **Dark Card Surface** | `#141414` | Bespoke project container & modal fills |
| **Electric Red Accent** | `#FF0000` | Primary hover border highlights, active indicators & badge fills |
| **High-Contrast White** | `#FFFFFF` | Monospaced display titles & headlines |
