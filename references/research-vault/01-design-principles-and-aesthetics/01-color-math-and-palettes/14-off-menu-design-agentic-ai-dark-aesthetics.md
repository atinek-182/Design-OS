# Off Menu Design — Agentic AI Pure Dark Aesthetics & Electric Blue Tokens

`Off Menu Design` (`https://www.offmenu.design/`) is an AI-native product studio founded by **Hunter Hammonds**, specializing in designing agentic interfaces and rapid startup web platforms.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Studio Pedigree

- **Founder**: Hunter Hammonds
- **Core Positioning**: AI-native product studio building agentic interfaces & high-velocity brand experiences.
- **Design Honors**: Featured on Framer Showcase (`buildinframer.com`), CSSline, Pafolios, and UI/UX Showcase.
- **Visual Persona**: Pure Dark canvas paired with Surface Charcoal elevation cards, Electric Blue highlight accents (`#3B82F6`), and an integrated Remi AI video avatar widget.

---

## Agentic Dark Palette Specification

| Color Role | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Pure Dark Canvas** | `#000000` | Viewport background fill |
| **Surface Charcoal Elevation** | `#0C0C0E` | Bento card containers & modal fills |
| **Electric Blue Accent** | `#3B82F6` | Primary hover indicators & active CTA highlights |
| **Soft Zinc Secondary** | `#71717A` | Subdued metadata text & border accents |
| **Crisp White Text** | `#FFFFFF` | Primary headline typography |
