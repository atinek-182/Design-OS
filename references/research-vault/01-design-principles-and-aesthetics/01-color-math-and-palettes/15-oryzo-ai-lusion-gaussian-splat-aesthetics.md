# Oryzo AI — Lusion 3D Gaussian Splatting & Satirical Orange Tokens

`Oryzo AI` (`https://oryzo.ai/`) is a WebGL 3D Gaussian Splatting flagship engineered by world-class digital production studio **Lusion** (`https://lusion.co`).

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Lusion Pedigree

- **Engineering Studio**: Lusion (Bristol / London, UK)
- **Core Technology**: 3D Gaussian Splatting WebGL Rendering & Rive WebAssembly Runtime
- **Visual Persona**: Pure Pitch Black background paired with Warm Cream surface typography, Satirical Orange callout accents (`#F97316`), and interactive 3D cork coaster physics.

---

## Satirical Product Color Specification

| Color Role | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Pitch Black Canvas** | `#000000` | Viewport background fill |
| **Warm Cream Surface** | `#FFEDD6` / `#FDE9CE` | Primary typography & Cork surface highlights |
| **Satirical Orange Accent** | `#F97316` | Warning modals, CTA buttons & prompt triggers |
| **Subdued Slate Border** | `#1C1C1E` | Card container borders & divider lines |
