# Made In Evolve — Italian Boutique Editorial Aesthetics & Neon Lime Tokens

`Made in Evolve` (`https://madeinevolve.com/`) is an Italian digital studio founded in 2008 in Modena, Italy, renowned for eCommerce innovation and high-contrast editorial design.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Studio Pedigree

- **Studio Location**: Modena, Italy (Est. 2008)
- **Honors**: Awwwards Honorable Mention & Digital Boutique Agency Nominee
- **Core Methodology**: *"Siamo nati developer. Il design ce lo siamo costruiti addosso. La tensione tra queste due anime è il nostro metodo."* (We were born developers. We built design around ourselves. The tension between these two souls is our method.)
- **Visual Persona**: Warm Off-White surface paired with Obsidian Black typography, Neon Lime highlight accents (`#C3FF00`), and pure white card panels.

---

## Italian Editorial Color Specification

| Color Role | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Off-White Surface** | `#F4F4F0` | Viewport background fill |
| **Obsidian Black Text** | `#0D0D0E` | High-contrast editorial titles & body copy |
| **Neon Lime Accent** | `#C3FF00` | Hover indicators, progress counters & CTA highlights |
| **Pure White Card** | `#FFFFFF` | Floating card panels & modal drawer fills |
