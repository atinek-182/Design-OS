# Sondaven — Ukrainian Luxury Resort Aesthetics & Gold Accent Palette

`Son Daven` (`https://sondaven.com/en`) is a luxury design resort hotel in Yaremche, Ukraine offering guaranteed investment returns.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Resort Pedigree

- **Location**: Yaremche, Ivano-Frankivsk Oblast, Ukraine
- **Core Concept**: Design resort hotel combining Carpathian mountain nature with high-yield investment opportunities
- **Visual Persona**: Ivory Light background (`#F6F4F0`) paired with Charcoal Dark typography (`#2C2824`), Gold Accents (`#A89474`), and Carpathian mountain cloud WebGL stages.

---
