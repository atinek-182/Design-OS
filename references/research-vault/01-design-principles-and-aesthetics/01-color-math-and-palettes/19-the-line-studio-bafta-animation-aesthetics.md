# The Line Studio — London BAFTA Animation Aesthetics & Electric Red Tokens

`THE LINE Studio` (`https://thelinestudio.com/`) is a BAFTA-nominated digital animation and craft studio based in London, UK.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Studio Pedigree

- **Location**: London, UK
- **Studio Pedigree**: BAFTA-nominated animation production studio creating world-class animated commercials, game trailers, and short films.
- **Visual Persona**: Electric Red canvas overlays (`#FF391E`) paired with Chalk White surfaces (`#F8F8F8`), Studio Grey borders (`#DDDEE2`), and Obsidian Charcoal display text (`#0B0B0B`).

---

## Animation Studio Color Specification

| Color Role | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Electric Red Canvas** | `#FF391E` | Preloader background, active route dots & acetate blend overlays |
| **Chalk White Surface** | `#F8F8F8` | Primary viewport background fill |
| **Studio Grey Divider** | `#DDDEE2` | Section divider lines & card borders |
| **Obsidian Charcoal Text** | `#0B0B0B` | High-contrast display typography |
