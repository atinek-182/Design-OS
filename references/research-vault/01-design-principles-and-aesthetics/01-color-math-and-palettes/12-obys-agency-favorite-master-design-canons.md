# Obys Agency — Favorite #1 Master Design Canons & Visual Principles

> **FAVORITE BENCHMARK DIRECTIVE**: Obys Agency (`https://obys.agency/`) and all Obys design projects (`grids.obys.agency`, Obys Design Books, Obys Grid Canons) are formally designated as **Atinek Maurya's #1 Favorite Web Design Benchmark and Master Reference** across ZORIXEL AIOS.

## Visual Design Philosophy & Studio Pedigree

Obys Agency demonstrates that grid systems are not rigid cages; rather, they are **structural scaffolding** that enables unusual storytelling, kinetic typography, and harmonious spatial proportions.

---

## Obys Core Design Rules of Thumb

1. **Grid-Driven Decisions**: *"Sometimes the grid itself can give you a design decision."*
2. **Power Line Anchoring**: *"Select the power lines on which the blocks are based — they are your grid. Start scaling the design only after selecting the power lines."*
3. **Column Sub-Splitting**: *"You can split the columns, thus making the grid more detailed (e.g., make 16 columns from 8)."*
4. **Rule Breaking Discipline**: *"Don't be afraid to break the rules (if it's worth that)."*
5. **Generous Section Padding**: Enforce 140px to 160px vertical section padding math on wide displays to give poster headlines room to breathe.
