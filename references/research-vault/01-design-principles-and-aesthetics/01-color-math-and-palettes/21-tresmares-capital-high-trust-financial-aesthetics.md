# Tresmares Capital — High-Trust Financial Aesthetics & Terracotta Peak Red

`Tresmares Capital` (`https://www.tresmarescapital.com/en/`) is an Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD, June 12 2026) winning private equity platform engineered by **Dgrees Studio** (Madrid, Spain).

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Studio Pedigree

- **Creative Agency**: Dgrees Studio (Madrid, Spain)
- **Honors**: Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD), Developer Award, Honorable Mention
- **Scale**: Managing over €3.5B+ in capital, backed by Santander Bank
- **Mountain Metaphor**: Design centers on the "Pico Tres" summit, representing steady growth, long-term capital preservation, and institutional strength.
- **Visual Persona**: Deep Obsidian background (`#0B0E14`) paired with Terracotta Peak Red (`#D94A38`), Metallic Gold (`#D4AF37`), and Light Slate Card Fills (`#131822`).
