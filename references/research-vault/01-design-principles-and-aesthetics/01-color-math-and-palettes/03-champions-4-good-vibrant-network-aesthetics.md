# Champions 4 Good — Vibrant Network Aesthetics & High-Energy Palette

`Champions for Good Club` by Double Play Studio is an Awwwards Developer Award winner (April 2026) bridging Olympic athletes, business leaders, and impact investors.

---

## Visual Design Architecture & Studio Pedigree

- **Awwwards Pedigree**: Developer Award Winner & Muzli Pick of the Day.
- **Design Persona**: High-energy impact network combining athletic dynamism with executive polish.
- **Playful Vitality**: Draggable interactive card stacks paired with bold uppercase typographic anchors.

## Vibrant Color Tokens & Identity Specification

| Color Role | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Off-White Canvas** | `#FAFAF8` | Primary body background fill |
| **Dark Charcoal Type** | `#0A0A0C` | Primary display headlines & body text |
| **Vibrant Pink Accent** | `#FF3366` | Primary Join Club CTA buttons, badges & active indicators |
| **Pure White Card Fill** | `#FFFFFF` | Membership card containers & interactive drag items |
