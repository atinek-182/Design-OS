# Locomotive Agency — Montréal Monochromatic Editorial Aesthetics

`Locomotive` (`https://locomotive.ca/en`) is a multiple Awwwards Agency of the Year, Site of the Year, and Developer of the Year-winning digital studio based in Montréal, Quebec, Canada.

---

## Visual Design Philosophy & Studio Pedigree

- **Honors**: Awwwards Agency of the Year, Site of the Year, Site of the Month, Developer of the Year.
- **Industry Legacy**: Creators of **Locomotive Scroll** — the open-source smooth scrolling JavaScript engine used globally across top-tier editorial and WebGL sites.
- **Visual Persona**: Pitch obsidian void background paired with crisp 1px borders, high-contrast crimson red highlights, and smooth inertia motion physics.

---

## Monochromatic Editorial Color Specification

| Color Role | Hex Code | Purpose & Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Obsidian Void Canvas** | `#0D0D0D` | Main body viewport background fill |
| **Charcoal Surface Elevation** | `#171717` | Card container fills & surface elevations |
| **High-Visibility Crimson Accent** | `#FF4757` | Primary hover states, active indicators & CTA accents |
| **Stark White Typography** | `#FFFFFF` | Bold display headlines & high-contrast body text |
| **Subdued Slate Border** | `#333333` | Crisp 1px structural container borders |
