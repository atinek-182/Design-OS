# Resn — 14-Time Agency of the Year Case Study & Blueprints

`Pioneer® — Corn Revolutionized` (`https://cornrevolution.resn.global/#`) is a WebGL biological showcase crafted by **Resn** (Wellington, New Zealand & Amsterdam, Netherlands).

---

## Platform Case Study Overview & Resn Pedigree

- **Target Site**: `https://cornrevolution.resn.global/#`
- **Agency**: Resn (New Zealand & Netherlands) × Bader Rutter
- **Agency Honors**: 14-time Awwwards Agency of the Year, Site of the Year (SOTY), FWA Hall of Fame
- **Core Technology**: Three.js WebGL particle engine, 6 custom GLSL fragment shaders, Howler.js velocity audio modulation
