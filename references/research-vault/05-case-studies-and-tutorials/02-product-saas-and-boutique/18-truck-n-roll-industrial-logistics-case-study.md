# Truck 'n Roll — Industrial Logistics Carrier Platform Case Study

`Truck 'n Roll` (`https://trucknroll.com/`) is an Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD, June 3 2026) winning digital platform engineered by **Locomotive®** (Montreal, QC, Canada).

---

## Platform Case Study Overview & Studio Pedigree

- **Target Site**: `https://trucknroll.com/`
- **Agency**: Locomotive® (Montreal, QC, Canada)
- **Honors**: Awwwards Site of the Day (SOTD, June 3 2026), Developer Award Winner
- **Pedigree**: 30-year North American live entertainment and stadium concert tour logistics carrier
- **Tech Stack**: Craft CMS, Vite JS, Locomotive ComponentManager, Swup.js SPA router, Lenis Scroll, Druk font stack
