# Follow Art — Contemporary Curator Psychology & Digital Card Identity

Follow Art uses principles of **Algorithm Autonomy** and **Physical-Digital Bridge Identity** to position their platform for art world professionals.

---

## Algorithm-Independent Framing Psychology

1. **Rebellious Positioning**: Framing the platform as an algorithm-independent network resonates strongly with artists and curators frustrated by social media algorithm changes.
2. **High-Contrast Boldness**: Using electric cobalt blue (`#0000FF`) signals uncompromised creative independence compared to traditional muted museum whites.

---

## "The Card" Mobile Wallet Infrastructure & Exhibition Networking

- **Physical-Digital Bridge**: "The Card" acts as a mobile digital wallet & QR code profile, allowing curators at physical art fairs (Art Basel, Venice Biennale) to instantly exchange portfolios with 1 tap.
- **Frictionless Sign Up Onboarding**: Clicking "Sign Up" opens an overlaid registration modal without breaking page scroll context.
