# Ashley Brooke — Tactile Drafting Psychology & High-Trust Conversion Choreography

Ashley Brooke CS leverages principles of **Architectural Authority** and **Action Positioning** to convert high-ticket design leads.

---

## Architectural Grid Line Psychology & Authority

1. **Blueprint Drafting Effect**: Exposing faint vertical grid lines (`.guide_column`) creates the psychological impression of a rigorous drafting studio rather than an off-the-shelf template.
2. **Serif & Grid Contrast**: Pairing organic serif typography with rigid grid lines balances artistic creativity with technical execution capability.

---

## Frictionless Contact Flow & Action Positioning

- **Action-First Mantra**: Prominently featuring *"Grounded in taking action, not just stopping at ideas"* reassures business founders that the agency delivers concrete outcomes.
- **Initiate Project CTA Modal**: Clicking "Initiate Project" in `NavHeader.tsx` triggers a streamlined email initiation modal directly in context, minimizing multi-step form drop-off.
