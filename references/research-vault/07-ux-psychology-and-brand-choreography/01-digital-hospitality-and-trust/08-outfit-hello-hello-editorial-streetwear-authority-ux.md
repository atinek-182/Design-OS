# Outfit Hello Hello — Editorial Streetwear Authority & Micro-Pill Button UX

Outfit Hello Hello applies **Oversized Typographic Authority** and **Tactile Micro-Pill Buttons** to elevate fashion brand presentation.

---

## Editorial Streetwear Authority Framing

1. **Magazine-Style Headline Scale**: Scaling display text to 45vw (`CREATIVITY IN MOTION`) mimics print fashion magazine covers, establishing instant design authority and luxury posture.
2. **High-Contrast Electric Color Blocking**: Juxtaposing Heritage Cream (`#EDE4DD`) against Electric Red (`#FF0001`) evokes high-fashion streetwear pop culture.

---

## Tactile Micro-Pill Button & Color Inversion Micro-Interactions

- **Tactile Pill Buttons**: Full-rounded pill CTA buttons (`rounded-full bg-[#FF0001]`) with micro-scale hover feedback (`scale-105`) invite clicks.
- **Custom Ring Cursor Inversion**: The custom cursor dot inverts colors over light/dark surfaces (`mix-blend-difference`), maintaining continuous visual contrast.
