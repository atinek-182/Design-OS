# Detroit Paris — Haute Couture Exclusivity Psychology & Video Choreography

Detroit Paris applies principles of **Luxury Exclusivity** and **Instant Motion Feedback** to position their generative AI studio at the top of the fashion industry.

---

## Haute Couture Exclusivity & Monochromatic Restraint

1. **Monochromatic Dark Authority**: Using pure dark (`#050505`) and high-contrast typography conveys elite fashion catalog authority, building immediate trust with luxury brands.
2. **Generative AI Sophistication**: Positioning AI as an art direction medium rather than a cheap automated tool shifts customer perception from low-cost vendor to high-ticket strategic partner.

---

## Instant Video Feedback & Direct Contact UX

- **Dynamic Video Hover Feedback**: Hovering over cards triggers instant video playback (`data-video-on-hover`), delivering immediate motion confirmation without extra clicks.
- **Direct Contact Navigation**: Clean header Contact CTA opens a dedicated inquiry flow for international brand campaigns.
