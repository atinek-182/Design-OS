# Gehry Getty — Archival Authority & Guided Audio Exhibition Psychology

Gehry Getty leverages **Guided Storytelling** and **Archival Institutional Trust** to guide users through complex architectural concepts.

---

## Institutional Archival Trust & Deconstructivist Storytelling

1. **Archival Integrity**: Displaying physical paper model creases and original hand sketches alongside 3D GLTF models establishes immense archival authenticity.
2. **Deconstructivist Narrative Structure**: Breaking down Frank Gehry's complex Walt Disney Concert Hall design into multi-chapter audio stories renders deconstructivist architecture approachable.

---

## Guided Audio Narration & Camera Panning Psychology

- **Passive & Active Dual Modes**: Users can passively listen to guided audio narratives (with automatic camera panning) or actively orbit and drag 3D models at their own pace.
- **Hotspot Pin Discovery**: Visual 3D hotspot pins (`.hotspot-pin`) invite curiosity and reward exploration with detailed architectural annotations.
