# Made In Evolve — Developer-Born Craft & Frictionless Conversion UX

Made In Evolve applies **Developer-Born Craft Framing** and **High-Contrast Micro-Interactions** to convert fashion and eCommerce clients.

---

## Developer-Born Craft Authority Framing

1. **"Developer First, Design Built Around Us"**: Openly positioning the studio as developers who mastered editorial design builds immense technical credibility, assuring clients that their custom sites will be robust and bug-free.
2. **Precision Italian Craft**: Muted off-white canvases paired with crisp black grid lines project artisan Modena craftsmanship.

---

## Neon Lime Micro-Interactions & Contact Drawer UX

- **Neon Lime Callouts**: High-visibility neon lime (`#C3FF00`) badges guide prospect focus directly to progress counters and active CTA triggers.
- **Frictionless Contact Drawer**: Top navigation Contact CTA opens a direct contact modal drawer without forcing full-page navigation context loss.
