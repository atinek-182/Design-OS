# Tresmares Capital — Pico Tres Mountain Metaphor & Investor Portal UX

Tresmares Capital applies **Institutional Peak Authority** and **High-Trust Financial Framing** to attract institutional investors.

---

## Pico Tres Mountain Summit Authority Framing

1. **Mountain Summit Metaphor**: Grounding visual design around mountain peaks ("Pico Tres") projects steady growth, long-term capital preservation, and unshakeable financial resilience.
2. **Santander Financial Heritage**: Displaying €3.5B+ capital metrics in metallic gold (`#D4AF37`) establishes immediate institutional credibility.

---

## High-Trust Investor Portal & Button Underline Offset UX

- **Fixed Investor Portal Access**: Top header features an explicit "Investor Portal" CTA button, reassuring institutional LPs with direct portal access.
- **Button Subrayado Underline Transitions**: Underline offset transitions (`text-underline-offset: 0.2em` to `0.5em`) provide refined feedback for navigation links.
