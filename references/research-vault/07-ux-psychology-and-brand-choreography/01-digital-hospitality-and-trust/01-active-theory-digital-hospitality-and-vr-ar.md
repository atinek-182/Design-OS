# Active Theory — Digital Hospitality, UX Psychology & Spatial Choreography

Active Theory applies core psychological principles of **Spatial Orientation** and **Digital Hospitality** to eliminate user anxiety when navigating complex 3D WebGL environments.

---

## UX Psychology & Spatial Orientation Mechanics

1. **Multisensory Feedback Loops**: Combining cursor vector particle dispersion with instantaneous Web Audio pitch sweeps provides tactile-like confirmation to user inputs without physical haptics.
2. **Predictable Mode Switching**: The global HUD mode switcher (`PORTFOLIO / VR / AR`) sets clear psychological expectations before entering immersive 3D viewports.
3. **Location Anchoring**: City HUD switcher (`LA / AMS`) grounds the global studio presence, building international credibility and high trust.

---

## Digital Hospitality & Non-Intrusive Frictionless CTAs

- **Non-Modal HUD Inquiry Drawer**: Instead of redirecting to a standalone `/contact` page or opening an aggressive pop-up modal, clicking `TALK` smoothly slides open a clean HUD drawer over the existing 3D stage.
- **Continuous Exploration**: Closing the inquiry drawer restores exact scroll and camera coordinates, ensuring uninterrupted project discovery.
