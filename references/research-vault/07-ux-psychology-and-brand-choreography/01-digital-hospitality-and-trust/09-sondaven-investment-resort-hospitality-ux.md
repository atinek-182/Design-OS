# Sondaven — Investment Resort Hospitality & Seasonal SVG Path UX

Sondaven applies **Luxury Investment Positioning** and **Interactive Seasonal SVG Toggles** to engage high-net-worth real estate investors.

---

## Investment Resort Authority Framing

1. **Guaranteed Return Positioning**: Explicitly presenting the resort hotel as an investment project with guaranteed yield (`Investment project with guaranteed return`) targets real estate buyers alongside resort guests.
2. **Carpathian Nature Heritage**: Muted ivory and gold tones paired with mountain cloud WebGL visuals establish authentic Carpathian luxury pedigree.

---

## Seasonal Summer/Winter SVG Path Toggle & Equalizer UX

- **Drag-Along-Path SVG Toggle**: Dragging the handle along the SVG curve toggles between Summer and Winter resort imagery, demonstrating year-round rental yield potential.
- **Carpathian Whispers Audio Feedback**: Smooth volume fading and animated equalizer bars provide an ambient soundscape without jarring visitors.
