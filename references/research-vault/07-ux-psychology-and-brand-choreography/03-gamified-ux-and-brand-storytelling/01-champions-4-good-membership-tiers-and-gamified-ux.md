# Champions 4 Good — Membership Tier Psychology & Gamified Network UX

Champions 4 Good uses **Gamified Interaction** and **Identity-Based Framing** to convert prospective members across four strategic tiers.

---

## Multi-Tier Membership Framing Psychology

1. **Role-Based Identity Segmentation**: Explicitly categorizing members into 4 distinct roles (Athlete, Booster, Business, Partner) gives visitors immediate self-identification.
2. **Schema.org Trust Architecture**: Embedding structured JSON-LD organization schema establishes immediate institutional authority for investors and corporate sponsors.

---

## Tactile Swipe Physics & Interactive Engagement

- **Tactile Card Manipulation**: Allowing users to drag and flick card stacks (`[data-flick-cards-dragger]`) creates a playful, hands-on sense of ownership over the content.
- **Frictionless Join Club Conversion**: Direct CTA buttons open Finsweet-powered registration modals without full-page navigation context switches.
