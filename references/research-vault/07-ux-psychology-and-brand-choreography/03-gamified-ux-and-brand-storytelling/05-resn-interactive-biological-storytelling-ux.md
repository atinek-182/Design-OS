# Resn — Interactive Biological Storytelling & Velocity Audio UX

Resn applies **Immersive Storytelling** and **Velocity-Modulated Spatial Audio** to transform B2B agricultural biotechnology into an emotional journey.

---

## Biological Biotech Narrative Framing

1. **Gamifying Complex Science**: Converting biological crop genetics into interactive 3D particle nodes allows visitors to feel like researchers exploring microscopic worlds.
2. **Surreal Cinematic Pacing**: Dividing the experience into 4 distinct chapters connected by 3D camera Z-axis translation creates cinematic narrative momentum.

---

## Scroll-Velocity Modulated Audio Feedback

- **Dynamic Audio Pitch & Volume Modulation**: As the user scrolls faster, the ambient hum modulates pitch higher and increases volume, giving instantaneous visceral feedback for scroll input.
- **Radial Particle Repulsion**: Moving the cursor across particle fields causes particles to disperse radially, providing tactile feedback that rewards cursor movement.
