# The Line Studio — Animation Craft Authority & FPS Preloader UX

THE LINE Studio applies **FPS Preloader Discipline** and **Card Tilt Video Reveals** to convert animation clients.

---

## Animation Craft FPS Preloader Framing

1. **FPS Counter as a Badge of Craft**: Displaying a live frames-per-second counter (`.preloader__fps`) in the preloader signals obsessive performance discipline, reassuring agency producers that the studio delivers 60FPS animation precision.
2. **Bold London Studio Persona**: High-contrast Electric Red (`#FF391E`) paired with crisp DenimVF typography establishes immediate British animation authority.

---

## Card Tilt Video Modal Reveals & Acetate Overlay UX

- **Card Tilt Video Modal Reveals**: Triggering video player modals with a subtle rotational tilt (`transform: translateY(-120%) rotate(-4deg)`) adds dynamic energy to video playback.
- **Red Acetate Multiply Overlays**: Blending electric red overlays (`mix-blend-mode: multiply`) over monochromatic imagery creates an iconic graphic signature.
