# Oryzo AI — Satirical Product Storytelling & Warning Modal UX

Oryzo AI applies **Satirical Humor** and **Interactive Prompt Widgets** to transform a simple cork coaster into a viral WebGL experience.

---

## Satirical Product Storytelling Copywriting

1. **High-Tech Treatment for Everyday Objects**: Treating a simple cork coaster with Apple-level 3D engineering and satirical copy ("Made for mugs. Built for tables.", "Designed to lift, insulate, and grip in all the right ways.") creates an unforgettable contrast that delights visitors.
2. **Humorous Disclaimers**: Warning modals ("This stunt was performed by professionals. Do not attempt this at home.") leverage playful reverse psychology to build social sharing momentum.

---

## Disclaimer Modal & Interactive Prompt Widget UX

- **Bikini Prompt Input Widget**: Features an interactive text input with an `@oryzo` tag and orange "Send" trigger button, giving users a direct input action.
- **Hand Pointer Magnetic Hover**: Interactive instruction text ("Try to hover hand") guides users to interact with the 3D hand mesh.
