# Merci Michel — Creativity Drives Technology & Gamified Audio UX

Merci Michel applies **Gamified Storytelling** and **Spatial Soundscape Feedback** to forge emotional brand connections.

---

## "Creativity Drives Technology" Narrative Framing

1. **Story First, Tech Second**: Framing technology as a vehicle for playful narrative storytelling ensures that 3D animations enhance rather than distract from brand messaging.
2. **French High-Craft Luxury**: Pairing gamified 3D elements with sleek obsidian and neon crimson aesthetics attracts luxury fashion and retail clients.

---

## Interactive Spatial Soundscape & 3D Tilt Micro-Interactions

- **Spatial Sound HUD Toggle**: Fixed header features an explicit audio toggle (`🔊 Sound: ON`), encouraging users to enable spatial audio for an immersive experience.
- **3D Card Tilt & Particle Bursts**: Hovering over project cards triggers dynamic 3D card tilt and particle burst effects, turning navigation into an interactive game.
