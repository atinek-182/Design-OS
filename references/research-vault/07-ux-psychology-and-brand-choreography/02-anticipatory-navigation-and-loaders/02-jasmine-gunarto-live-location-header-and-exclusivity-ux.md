# Jasmine Gunarto — Live Location Header & High-Fashion Exclusivity UX

Jasmine Gunarto applies **Global Presence Framing** and **High-Fashion Exclusivity** to position the studio for elite commercial brand commissions.

---

## Real-Time Location Header & Global Presence Framing

1. **Live Clock & Timezone Display**: Displaying real-time local time (`NEW YORK, US`), timezone, and GPS coordinates directly in the sticky top header communicates international availability and operational transparency.
2. **Minimalist Exclusivity**: Sparse editorial typography and muted earth brown accents project luxury studio restraint.

---

## Embla Popup Slider Carousel Modal UX

- **Full-Screen Carousel Drawer**: Selecting a project opens a full-screen Embla/Swiper carousel modal (`.popup-slider`), allowing prospects to immerse themselves in motion design breakdowns without losing page state.
- **Close Button Ergonomics**: Prominent SVG `.btn-close` icon ensures effortless modal dismissal.
