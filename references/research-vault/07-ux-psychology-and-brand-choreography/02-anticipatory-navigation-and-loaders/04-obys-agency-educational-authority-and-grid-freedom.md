# Obys Agency — Educational Authority Psychology & Grid Freedom UX

Obys Agency applies principles of **Educational Authority** and **Scaffolding Framing** to build international acclaim and attract top-tier global clients.

---

## Educational Authority & Scaffolding Framing Psychology

1. **Scaffolding vs Cage Framing**: Reframing grids as *structural scaffolding* rather than rigid constraints eliminates designer resistance, establishing Obys as thought leaders in web layout design.
2. **Generous Educational Openness**: Freely sharing complete layout formulas (12/10/8 columns, Van De Graaff diagonal lines, baseline grid math) builds immense trust and brand affinity.

---

## Frictionless Learning UX & Grid Overlay Discovery

- **Interactive Grid Overlay**: Allowing users to toggle grid lines on and off directly over live web design examples provides instant visual proof of the underlying grid math.
- **Asymmetrical Storytelling**: Demonstrating how power lines anchor asymmetrical headline placements inspires confidence in high-contrast poster typography layout.
