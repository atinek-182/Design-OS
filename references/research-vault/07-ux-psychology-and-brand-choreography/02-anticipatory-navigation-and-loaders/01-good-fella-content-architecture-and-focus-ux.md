# Good Fella — Content Architecture Psychology & Tab Focus UX

Good Fella applies principles of **Open-Source Authority** and **Re-engagement Micro-Interactions** to capture and retain high-value brand clients.

---

## Open-Source Frontend Authority Framing

1. **Industry Component Authority**: Publishing **Annnimate** and showcasing **The Content Architecture** on Codrops establishes Julian & Adrian Fella as master engineers, attracting clients seeking elite technical execution.
2. **Monospaced Precision**: Utilizing monospaced `Geist Mono` typography reinforces a precise, developer-first engineering aesthetic.

---

## Tab Title Attention Capture & Modal Overlay UX

- **Dynamic Tab Title Swapper**: Swaps the document title when the user switches tabs, drawing immediate psychological attention back to the site.
- **Modal Overlay System**: `ModalProvider` & `ModalOverlay` provide seamless contact and project detail drawers without full-page navigation context loss.
