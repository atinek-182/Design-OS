# Obys Agency — Awwwards SOTY & SOTM Master Rubric Benchmark

As ZORIXEL's **#1 Favorite Web Design Benchmark**, Obys Agency represents the gold standard for jury scoring across Awwwards Site of the Year (SOTY) and Site of the Month (SOTM) competitions.

---

## Technical Performance & Grid Discipline Safeguards

1. **Margin & Gutter Lock**: Enforces strict `30px` mobile, `60px` tablet, `80px` desktop side margins with constant `20px` gutters.
2. **Baseline Integer Multiplication**: All vertical paddings, margins, and font line-heights are exact integer multiples of the baseline unit ($16\text{px} \times 1.5 = 24\text{px}$).
3. **SVG Noise Performance**: SVG noise turbulence is rendered on a separate hardware-accelerated layer to preserve 60 FPS scroll performance.
