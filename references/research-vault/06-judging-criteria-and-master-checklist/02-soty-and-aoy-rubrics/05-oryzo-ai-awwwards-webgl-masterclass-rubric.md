## Mobile Viewport Height Lock & PBR Map Safeguards

1. **`--vh` Mobile Height Lock**: Custom CSS variable calculated on `resize` to prevent mobile address bar expanding/collapsing viewport shifts.
2. **Multi-Channel PBR Texture Suite**: Ingests compressed AVIF and WebP textures (`HEIGHT.webp`, `cork_closeup.webp`, `gobo_b.avif`) for hyper-realistic lighting.
