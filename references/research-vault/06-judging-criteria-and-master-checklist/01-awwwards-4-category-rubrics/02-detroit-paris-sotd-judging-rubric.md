## Technical Performance & Video Streaming Safeguards

1. **HLS.js Adaptive Bitrate Switching**: Automatically downgrades video resolution on slower connections to eliminate buffering spikes.
2. **Scrollbar Suppression**: Hidden WebKit scrollbars create a seamless full-viewport cinematic canvas.
3. **WebFont Optimization**: Preconnects to Google Fonts CDN to render Barlow text styles without FOIT/FOUT font flickering.
