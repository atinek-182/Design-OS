## 🧬 5-Layer Site DNA Analysis

### Layer 1: Executive Brand & Aesthetic Identity
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Italian digital boutique identity featuring off-white background (`#F4F4F0`), obsidian black text (`#0D0D0E`), lime neon accent highlights (`#C3FF00`), and 3D WebGL shader canvases.
- **Brand Position:** "Direction, Tech, Marketing for Visionary Brands. Born developers, self-built designers."
- **Color Palette:** Off-White (`#F4F4F0`), Obsidian Black (`#0D0D0E`), Neon Lime (`#C3FF00`), Muted Slate (`#666660`).
- **Typography Stack:** `Neue Haas Grotesk Text` Typekit Font (`tk-neue-haas-grotesk-text`).

### Layer 2: UX Structure & Layout Engineering
- **Viewport System:** Responsive layout scaling from 1920px down to 375px.
- **Loader Grid System:** Multi-cell grid loader transition (`loader__cell`).
- **SPA Architecture:** Barba.js transition wrappers (`data-barba="wrapper"`).

### Layer 3: Frontend Stack & Engineering Frameworks
- **3D Canvas Engine:** Unicorn Studio WebGL Engine 2.0.3 (`unicornStudio.umd.js`).
- **Animation Suite:** GSAP 3.12 (Flip, ScrambleText, SplitText, ScrollTrigger, CustomEase).
- **Text Curved Engine:** CircleType (`circletype.min.js`).

### Layer 4: Interactive Micro-Interactions & Motion Mechanics
- **Grid Cell Transition:** Cellular mask animation sequence upon page load.
- **Counter Increment:** Dynamic progress bar indicator with lime neon color.
