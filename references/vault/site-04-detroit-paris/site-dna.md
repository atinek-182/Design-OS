## 🧬 5-Layer Site DNA Analysis

### Layer 1: Executive Brand & Aesthetic Identity
- **Visual Aesthetic:** High-fashion luxury AI production identity featuring pure dark background (`#050505`), charcoal card surfaces (`#0E0E10`), high-contrast white typography (`#FFFFFF`), and 4K HLS video stream backgrounds.
- **Brand Position:** "AI production house in Paris crafting visual culture for luxury brands."
- **Color Palette:** Pure Dark (`#050505`), Charcoal Surface (`#0E0E10`), High-Contrast White (`#FFFFFF`), Muted Slate (`#888888`).
- **Typography Stack:** `Barlow` Google Font (weights 300, 400, 500, 600, 700).

### Layer 2: UX Structure & Layout Engineering
- **Viewport System:** Responsive layout scaling from 1920px down to 375px.
- **Micro-Typography Scaling:** Osmo Supply typography formula (`font-size: calc(0rem + 1vw)`).
- **Navigation:** Header bar with direct links to Artists, Work, Talent, and Contact.

### Layer 3: Frontend Stack & Engineering Frameworks
- **Video Streaming Pipeline:** HLS.js 1.6.11 (`hls.js@1.6.11`).
- **Platform Engine:** Webflow CMS with custom JS bundle (`main.js` 339.8 KB).
- **Scroll Engine:** Lenis Smooth Scroll (`class="lenis"`).

### Layer 4: Interactive Micro-Interactions & Motion Mechanics
- **Video Hover Trigger:** `data-video-on-hover` attribute initiating HLS video stream playback on card hover.
- **Masonry Grid Math:** Flexible column masonry grid system (`data-masonry-list`).
