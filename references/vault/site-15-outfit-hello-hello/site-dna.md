## 🧬 5-Layer Site DNA Analysis

### Layer 1: Executive Brand & Aesthetic Identity
- **Visual Aesthetic:** High-impact editorial streetwear fashion showcase built around oversized bold typography, high-contrast cream/red color blocks, and kinetic scroll transformations.
- **Color Palette:** Heritage Cream (`#EDE4DD`), Electric Red (`#FF0001`), Jet Black (`#000000`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Typography Stack:** `Neue Haas Grotesk Text Pro` (Bold 900 / Medium 700 / Normal 400).

### Layer 2: UX Structure & Layout Engineering
- **Viewport System:** Responsive layout scaling from Desktop (1920px) to Mobile (375px).
- **Navigation Mechanics:** Fixed top navigation with brand logo tag (`OUTFIT // ++HELLOHELLO`) and pill CTA button.
- **Scroll Transform Layers:** Image card rotation and scale transform triggered on scroll.

### Layer 3: Frontend Stack & Engineering Frameworks
- **Framework & Bundler:** Next.js 14+ with Turbopack bundler (`turbopack-3882f364d49ff303.js`).
- **Styling System:** Tailwind CSS v4 (`@layer theme`, `@layer utilities`).
- **Motion Engine:** Framer Motion paired with Lenis inertia scroll.

### Layer 4: Interactive Micro-Interactions & Motion Mechanics
- **Custom Cursor:** Custom cursor ring (`.has-custom-cursor`).
- **Image Preloader Stream:** Responsive Next.js Image preloading with `srcset` optimization.
