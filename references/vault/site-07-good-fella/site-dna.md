## 🧬 5-Layer Site DNA Analysis

### Layer 1: Executive Brand & Aesthetic Identity
- **Visual Aesthetic:** High-contrast digital landmark identity featuring obsidian dark background (`#0A0A0A`), pure white typography (`#FFFFFF`), electric red accent highlights (`#FF0000`), and Geist Mono font formatting.
- **Brand Position:** "The digital landmark studio. We build the singular online expression of consumer, sports and entertainment brands."
- **Color Palette:** Obsidian Dark (`#0A0A0A`), Card Surface (`#141414`), Electric Red (`#FF0000`), Muted Slate (`#888888`).
- **Typography Stack:** `Geist Mono` font family (`geistmono_3b3e651d-module__HiZKDa__variable`).

### Layer 2: UX Structure & Layout Engineering
- **Viewport System:** Responsive layout scaling from 1920px down to 375px.
- **CMS Architecture:** Sanity CMS Content Architecture (`cdn.sanity.io`) with preloaded image responsive srcsets.
- **Grid Column Overlay:** Multi-bar grid layout transition overlay (`bg-brand/10`).

### Layer 3: Frontend Stack & Engineering Frameworks
- **Framework & Compiler:** Next.js 14+ with Turbopack SSR (`turbopack-a9293ee6befd07a1.js`).
- **Analytics & Telemetry:** PostHog telemetry integration (`r.good-fella.com/array/...`).
- **Scroll Engine:** Lenis Smooth Scroll (`lenis.min.js`).

### Layer 4: Interactive Micro-Interactions & Motion Mechanics
- **Grid Column Transition:** Staggered vertical scale transitions (`transform: scaleY(0)`).
- **Red Highlight Hover:** Electric red border state transitions on project cards.
