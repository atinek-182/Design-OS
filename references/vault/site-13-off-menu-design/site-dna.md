## 🧬 5-Layer Site DNA Analysis

### Layer 1: Executive Brand & Aesthetic Identity
- **Visual Aesthetic:** AI-native product studio identity featuring a pure dark background (`#000000`), electric blue accents (`#3B82F6`), high-contrast typography, and floating 3D portfolio spheres.
- **Brand Position:** "Product studio designing agentic interfaces. AI-accelerated exploration meets expert judgment to ship faster."
- **Color Palette:** Pure Dark (`#000000`), Surface Charcoal (`#0C0C0E`), Electric Blue (`#3B82F6`), Muted Zinc (`#71717A`).

### Layer 2: UX Structure & Layout Engineering
- **Viewport System:** Responsive layout scaling across 5 viewports (1920px to 375px).
- **Embedded Remi AI Assistant:** Floating chat widget (`#chat-window-position`) with automated video avatar (`/images/remi_dark@2x.mp4`).
- **3D Orbital Work Stage:** `data-sphere-index` rotating portfolio carousel.

### Layer 3: Frontend Stack & Engineering Frameworks
- **Framework & Compiler:** Next.js 14+ with Turbopack SSR compiler (`turbopack-25760bca079a3c46.js`).
- **Styling Tokens:** Tailwind CSS v4 design system.
- **Analytics & Tracking:** Plausible privacy-first analytics (`pa-LIF4z4gbZTrAXEtinVA1Q.js`).

### Layer 4: Interactive Micro-Interactions & Motion Mechanics
- **Difference Cursor Physics:** `mix-blend-difference` custom cursor trailing mouse coordinates.
- **Rotational Orbital Math:** CSS container queries (`container-type: inline-size`) with dynamic `--rotation-deg` parameters.
