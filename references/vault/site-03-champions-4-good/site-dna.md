## 🧬 5-Layer Site DNA Analysis

### Layer 1: Executive Brand & Aesthetic Identity
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Vibrant, playful digital network identity featuring off-white background (`#FAFAF8`), dark charcoal text (`#0A0A0C`), hot pink accent buttons (`#FF3366`), and draggable physics cards.
- **Brand Position:** "Connecting Olympic-level athletes, entrepreneurs, and investors through sports events and community."
- **Color Palette:** Off-White (`#FAFAF8`), Dark Charcoal (`#0A0A0C`), Hot Pink (`#FF3366`), Slate Muted (`#707075`).

### Layer 2: UX Structure & Layout Engineering
- **Viewport System:** Responsive layout scaling from 1920px down to 375px.
- **Root Scale Math:** Dynamic viewport width variable (`--vw: 19.2px`).
- **Membership Tiers:** Structured pricing cards (Athlete, Booster €85/mo, Business €150/mo, Partner).

### Layer 3: Frontend Stack & Engineering Frameworks
- **App Platform Engine:** Vercel App Engine (`champions4good.vercel.app/scripts/app.js`).
- **Physics Canvas:** Flick Cards Draggable Physics (`[data-flick-cards-dragger]`).
- **Analytics Engine:** Umami Privacy-First Analytics (`cloud.umami.is`).

### Layer 4: Interactive Micro-Interactions & Motion Mechanics
- **Draggable Cards Physics:** Touch & mouse drag listeners intercepting card stacks.
- **Momentum Inertia Scroll:** Lenis smooth scrolling integration.
