## 🧬 5-Layer Site DNA Analysis

### Layer 1: Executive Brand & Aesthetic Identity
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Boutique creative consultancy aesthetic combining matte dark backgrounds (`#0D0D0E`) with warm terracotta red accents (`#E0533C`), editorial serif typography, and tactile grid lines.
- **Brand Tagline:** "Grounded in taking action, not just stopping at ideas."
- **Color Palette:** Matte Dark (`#0D0D0E`), Card Charcoal (`#161618`), Terracotta Red (`#E0533C`), Warm Cream (`#F5F3EF`).
- **Typography Stack:** Classical Serif Display paired with technical sans-serif body text.

### Layer 2: UX Structure & Layout Engineering
- **Viewport System:** Responsive layout scaling from Desktop (1920px) to Mobile (375px).
- **Barba.js SPA Routing:** Seamless route transitions without full document reloads (`data-barba="wrapper"`).
- **Navigation:** Sticky top bar with action CTA button (`Initiate Project`).

### Layer 3: Frontend Stack & Engineering Frameworks
- **Platform:** Webflow CMS platform.
- **Animation Suite:** GSAP 3.13 (with SplitText, DrawSVGPlugin, CustomEase, and ScrollTrigger).
- **Scroll Engine:** Lenis Smooth Scroll (`lenis.min.js`).
- **Slider Runtime:** Swiper 12 (`swiper-bundle.min.js`).

### Layer 4: Interactive Micro-Interactions & Motion Mechanics
- **SplitText Typography Reveal:** Character and word staggered entrance animations.
- **Card Hover Physics:** Smooth scale expansion (`scale: 1.02`) on portfolio items.
