## 🧬 5-Layer Site DNA Analysis

### Layer 1: Executive Brand & Aesthetic Identity
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Editorial investment banking elegance inspired by mountain peak "Pico Tres". Dark obsidian background (`#0B0E14`) paired with rich terracotta red (`#D94A38`) and metallic gold (`#D4AF37`) metric accents.
- **Typography Stack:** 
  - Serif Display: `PP Fragment Serif`
  - Sans-Serif Utility: `PP Neue Montreal`

### Layer 2: UX Structure & Layout Engineering
- **Viewport System:** Responsive layout scaling across Desktop (1920px), Laptop (1440px), Tablet (1024px/768px), and Mobile (375px).
- **Navigation Mechanics:** Fixed top header with language switcher (`EN` / `ES`) and direct link to Investor Portal.
- **Taxi.js SPA Architecture:** `data-taxi` and `data-taxi-view` containers preventing page reload flickers.

### Layer 3: Frontend Stack & Engineering Frameworks
- **Framework & CMS:** WordPress engine optimized by WP Rocket (`wp-theme-tresmares`).
- **SPA Routing Engine:** Taxi.js (`data-taxi`, `data-taxi-view="default"`).
- **Script Hydration:** RocketLazyLoadScripts (`RocketLazyLoadScripts.run()`).
- **Multilingual System:** WPML (`wpml-cookie-js`, `hreflang="en"`, `hreflang="es"`).

### Layer 4: Interactive Micro-Interactions & Motion Mechanics
- **Scroll Engine:** Lenis inertia smooth scroll (`class="lenis"`).
- **Metrics Counter:** GSAP scroll-triggered counter for assets under management (€3.5B+).
