## 🧬 5-Layer Site DNA Analysis

### Layer 1: Executive Brand & Aesthetic Identity
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Editorial motion designer showcase pairing high-contrast Obsidian Black (`#090909`) with Off-White Cream (`#EBEAE4`) and Earth Brown (`#341C09`) footer accents.
- **Micro Meta Widget:** Header bar displaying real-time location (`NEW YORK, US`), live clock (`1:17 AM GMT-4`), and geographical coordinates (`36.7783° N, 119.4179°`).
- **Typography Stack:** Classical Editorial Serif Display paired with `Manrope` Sans-Serif body text.

### Layer 2: UX Structure & Layout Engineering
- **Viewport System:** Responsive layout scaling from Desktop (1920px) down to Mobile (375px).
- **Taxi.js SPA Architecture:** `data-taxi` and `data-taxi-view` page transitions with preloader numeric counter (`001` to `100`).
- **Navigation:** Multi-state view swapper (`Home`, `Works`, `Break`, `About`).

### Layer 3: Frontend Stack & Engineering Frameworks
- **Framework & Build Engine:** Vite build engine + LiteSpeed Cache optimization + WordPress backend (`theme-jasmine`).
- **Animation Suite:** GSAP 3 (ScrollTrigger, SplitText, PinSpacer, ClipPath Inset).
- **Slider Runtime:** Embla & Swiper slider modals (`popup-slider`).

### Layer 4: Interactive Micro-Interactions & Motion Mechanics
- **Kinetic SplitText Stagger:** Character-level split text transitions on section headlines.
- **Interactive Cursor Physics:** Magnetic button effects and hover cursor expansions.
