## 🧬 5-Layer Site DNA Analysis

### Layer 1: Executive Brand & Aesthetic Identity
- **Visual Aesthetic:** Rebellious contemporary art platform identity featuring electric cobalt blue background (`#000000FF`), pitch black surfaces (`#000000`), crisp white typography (`#FFFFFF`), and 3D WebGL shader canvases.
- **Brand Position:** "Digital infrastructure for curators & artists. Intentional discovery for contemporary art across 100+ countries."
- **Color Palette:** Electric Cobalt (`#0000FF`), Obsidian Black (`#000000`), Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Soft Periwinkle (`#A3A3FF`).
- **Typography Stack:** `HeadingNow` & `Hardbop` Font Stacks.

### Layer 2: UX Structure & Layout Engineering
- **Viewport System:** Responsive layout scaling from 1920px down to 375px (`viewport-fit=cover`).
- **Promo Header Component:** Fixed header transition bar (`.promo-header`, `.promo-header--expanded`).
- **Digital Portfolio Card System:** "The Card" digital portfolio integration.

### Layer 3: Frontend Stack & Engineering Frameworks
- **Framework & Compiler:** Vite + React 18 SPA (`ADt8-e4c.js`).
- **3D Canvas Engine:** Vide Infra Custom 3D WebGL Shader Pipeline (`landing-1-intro-webgl__canvas-wrapper`).
- **Scroll Engine:** Lenis Smooth Scroll (`lenis.min.js`).

### Layer 4: Interactive Micro-Interactions & Motion Mechanics
- **Sticky Section Mechanics:** Sticky section layers (`.section__layer--sticky`).
- **Button Hover Accent:** Scale transform hover accent lines (`.btn__hover-accent`).
