Fonts, small

settings & paring

1

Determine 
the purpose and nature

of the project

The choice of fonts depends on the context
of a project, on the branding and emotions that the designer wants to convey. Neutral or display, serif or sans serif, classic or futuristic. The font is the soul of the project.

Basically, we choose 1–2 fonts for our projects.
Examples of combining project theme and fonts:

classic Fashion – serif (Bodoni, Didot...)

JEWELRY – SERIF (CANELA, SILK, Saol...)

BANKING – neutral sans serif (Open sans, PT Sans)

Architecture – Geometric sans serif (Helvetica, Formular...)

There is an exception to each rule and the font choice will depend not only on the industry, 
but also on the project/brand.

2

Line-height 
& Tracking 

Line-height and tracking will depend on the font, its size and purpose 
(text for reading, text for headings...).


For Example:

Paragraph / Text

size – 14pt (px), line-height – 120%–130%, tracking – (-1%)–(+1%)

Header / H1

size – 250pt (px), line-height – 90%–100%, tracking – (-6%)–(0%)

This is just an example and each font comes 
with its own standard settings. But in almost every project we adjust these characteristics for ourselves.


Saol Display (Regular)

size – 250pt (px), line-height – 90%–100%, tracking – (-4,5%)

3

Font Pairs

Often we do projects in which we use one font.  But to give typography more contrast, emphasis and character we use font pairs – a combination  of two different fonts.

 Example: for large headings we use a serif font, for text – Sans Serif. When combining fonts,
 you need to follow several rules:

Сontrast – Different types of fonts (sans serif and serif)

Proportions – choose fonts with similar proportions

Font details – descenders, serif shapes etc.
