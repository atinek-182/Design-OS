# Qualitative User Interview Protocol & Synthesis

## 1. Interview Guide Structure
1. **Warm-up (5 mins)**: Context, rapport, and background.
2. **Core Exploration (30 mins)**: Unpack specific past behaviors rather than hypothetical wishes.
3. **Deep Dive / Edge Cases (15 mins)**: Pain points, workarounds, and emotional triggers.
4. **Wrap-up (5 mins)**: Participant open feedback and debrief.

## 2. Transcript Synthesis
- Extract verbatim user quotes highlighting friction.
- Group observations into affinity thematic clusters.
- Map findings to functional and emotional Jobs to Be Done.
